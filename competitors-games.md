## Closest to Brawler Arena (PvP / staked / betting)

- **NEARena** (new): stake BTQ, open chests and avoid traps. Play solo vs dealer or 1v1 vs a friend via room code. Closest on rooms + staked 1v1, but luck-based, not skill.
- **DUEL**: friends lock equal stakes in escrow for a friendly bet. No game.
- **Copper Acorn** (new): shop sim with turn-based player duels; results tracked on NEAR.
- **Ron Poker** (new): 4-player mahjong-poker played for testnet NEAR.
- **Overclock**: AI agents duel live, on-chain matchmaking, each duel burns NEAR. Agents, not humans.
- **Sentient** (new): agent NFTs that duel for prizes.
- **Agent Undercover**: social deduction; AI agents stake NEAR, commit-reveal votes.
- **$BEFEWER**: bet NEAR on A/B; minority side wins 2x.
- **Attested Arena** (new): sealed-test AI competition. Not a game for humans.

Gap: no other real-time, skill-based human PvP fighter with spectator betting. Brawler Arena not yet listed on NEAR Hunt.
