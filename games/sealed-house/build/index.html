Bid blind. Open the vault. Cash out or keep it.

Each lot is a sealed vault. Its contents were rolled by MagicBlock VRF, and you get three hints: its weight (junk is heavy, gems are light), one item shown face up, and how many items glow (rare or better).

Seal one bid against three rival collectors. Highest bid wins and pays; ties go to VRF. Sell items back to the house at 90% of listed value or keep them. 5% of each winning bid goes to the house fee vault.

5 lots, 💰1000 to start. Final bankroll + kept items (at buyback) = score.
